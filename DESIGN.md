# Bakervaughn: "The Pass"

## Thesis
Build the system that runs your business. The business is shown as a live pass: calls, enquiries, stock, staff alerts land as tickets under the heat lamp and get handled by one connected system.
Refuses the AI default: violet/indigo glow, cyan "live" dots, Inter/Geist, three rounded cards, floating orbs.

## Own world
- Ground: warm dark steel (not blue-black). Surfaces are brushed steel tiers.
- Paper: thermal ticket paper, printed in mono ink. The only light surface on the page.
- Lamp: heat-lamp amber. Means LIVE / ANSWERED / primary action. Never decoration.
- Docket red: stamps and urgent states only.
- Type: Archivo (variable, widened for display, tight tracking) + JetBrains Mono. The mono appears ONLY where real systems print: tickets, boards, timestamps.

lamp oklch(79% 0.155 68) / lamp-deep 66% / docket oklch(56% 0.19 29)
Spacing: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 80 / 128. Radius: 2px controls, 4px panels. Hairline before shadow.

## Story
Problem (unanswered enquiries, scattered data, compliance scramble) → five services on one menu → one connected system → proof (work) → the operators → how we work → Labs → why us → book a free review.

## Motion
One authored moment per surface. Hero: tickets print (stepped clip reveal) and get stamped. Connect: pinned map, packets travel the lines on scroll. Close: split-flap office board. Everything else: quiet reveals. Ease cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). Reduced motion: static states, no Lenis.

## Do not
No purple, cyan, gradient text, glass, glow orbs. No mono as costume outside printed artefacts. No icon-card grids. No invented clients, prices or results: placeholders stay visible as [ADD ...]. Sample tickets are labelled synthetic.
